# 🚀 NoFap Tracker App

A privacy-focused, minimalist habit tracker designed to help users track their streaks, build discipline, and achieve their personal growth goals. 

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Live Streak Counter:** Tracks days, hours, minutes, and seconds since your last milestone.
* **Milestone & Badges System:** Earn visual rewards and motivation at key milestones (3 days, 1 week, 30 days, 90 days).
* **Relapse Analytics & Journaling:** Log triggers and moods to understand patterns and prevent future relapses.
* **Daily Motivation:** Receive curated inspirational quotes and tips to stay strong during urges.
* **Privacy First:** All data is stored locally on the device. No data tracking, no cloud syncing (unless opted in).
* **Panic Button:** One-tap access to emergency resources, breathing exercises, or a distracting mini-game when urges hit.
